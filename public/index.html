Why detailed rental information matters

How much will I actually pay?

Rent, deposit, maintenance and other charges are shown separately — never buried in a description.

Does the owner live here?

Same building, same floor or elsewhere — and whether the entrance is separate or shared.

How does electricity work?

Separate meter, sub-meter or shared? And how the bill is split, so there are no surprises.

Who can live here?

Family, bachelors, students or working professionals — plus occupant limits, pets and visitors.

Water and parking

Water source and known shortages, and the number of car and bike spaces.

Honest labels

Direct owner, property manager or broker — clearly marked. No fake ratings or made-up verification.
